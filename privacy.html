Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This policy explains how OMB Autopilot ("the app"), operated by One Million Bucks, handles data. The app is internal software used only to publish One Million Bucks videos to accounts owned by One Million Bucks.

Who uses the app

Only the One Million Bucks account owner. The app does not offer sign-in to the public and does not collect information from viewers.

Data the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos to our own channel and to read basic channel and video information (channel name, video IDs, view, like and comment counts).
  • Facebook and Instagram (Meta): permission to publish Reels to our own Facebook Page and linked Instagram professional account, and to read basic insights for those posts.
  • TikTok: permission to send videos to our own TikTok account and read basic statistics for our own videos.

How the data is used

Only to upload our own videos, confirm that each upload succeeded, avoid duplicate posts, and measure how our own videos perform. Data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in our private automation environment and are never published. Post IDs, upload statuses and performance counts are kept in a private database. Tokens can be revoked at any time from each platform's account settings.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data

Revoking the app's access in Google, Meta or TikTok account settings stops all access immediately. Stored post IDs and statistics for our own videos are deleted on request or when the project ends.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact options on the One Million Bucks YouTube channel or Facebook Page.
